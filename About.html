About Us

Masia Maths and Science Academy (MMSA) is a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering rural learners through innovative teaching and quality STEM education. Located in Masia Village, Limpopo, MMSA provides a beacon of hope for underprivileged learners who aspire to excel in Mathematics, Physical Sciences, and ICT.

As a community-focused initiative, MMSA collaborates with local schools, including Ramauba Secondary School, to deliver life-changing educational support. By combining a passion for teaching with state-of-the-art resources, we are reshaping the academic landscape and bridging the gap between rural and urban education.

Vision

To inspire a lifelong passion for Mathematics and Science, cultivating a positive attitude and engagement with STEM subjects among learners.

Mission

To provide comprehensive academic support that enhances content knowledge and inquiry skills in Mathematics, Physical Sciences, and ICT. MMSA is committed to increasing enrolment and success in STEM-related fields, preparing learners for tertiary education and future careers.

Transforming Education, Changing Lives

Strategic Objectives

Ignite Passion for STEM

Ignite a passion for Mathematics and Physical Sciences from an early age to increase enrolment in grade 10.

Raise the pass rate

Raise the pass rate to over 60%, thus enhancing the number of students pursuing STEM at tertiary institutions.

Provide Tutors

Provide dedicated and passionate tutors to support learners in their academic journeys.

Boost Performance

Implement strategies to boost learners' performance within Masia and its surroundings.

Promote Reading Culture

Promote a reading culture to minimize crime and encourage academic excellence.

Develop STEM Infrastructure

Develop STEM infrastructure to foster innovation among learners.

Transform schools

Transform a public high school into a sciencefocused institution

Our Core Values

At MMSA, our work is guided by six foundational values:

Accountability

Taking full responsibility for our actions and outcomes to ensure the best for our learners and community.

Ethics

Doing what is right, even when faced with challenges, and maintaining the highest standards of conduct.

Transparency

Operating with honesty and openness in all our activities to build trust with students, parents, and stakeholders.

Integrity

Upholding strong moral codes and ethical convictions in every decision and action we take.

Empowerment

Equipping learners with the tools and confidence to overcome obstacles and achieve their potential.

Sustainability

Designing programs that deliver long-term impact and foster continuous growth in education.
