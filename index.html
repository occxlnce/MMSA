Our Journey

We are committed to transforming education in rural communities. Since 2018, MMSA has been at the forefront of fostering excellence in Mathematics and Science among learners in Masia Village and surrounding areas.
Our academy provides a beacon of hope, equipping students with the knowledge and skills they need to succeed in STEM fields and empowering them to overcome the challenges of socio-economic disadvantages.

ICT Academy

Intervention Programme

Providing additional support in Mathematics, Physical Sciences, and Life Sciences to close learning gaps.

STEM Girl Child Programme

Empowering young girls with mentorship opportunities and exposure to female role models in STEM.

Career Guidance

Helping learners explore career paths, tertiary education, and self-development opportunities.

What we do

Breaking Barriers Through Education

At Masia Maths and Science Academy, we believe education is the most powerful tool to break the cycle of poverty. By equipping learners with skills in Mathematics, Physical Sciences, and ICT, we are creating opportunities for a brighter future.

Where we work

Empowering Rural Communities in South Africa

Masia Maths and Science Academy operates in the heart of Masia Village, extending its impact to surrounding rural areas in Limpopo. By focusing on underserved communities, we are bridging educational gaps and creating opportunities for learners to thrive.

Our Core Values

Service beyond self

We are dedicated to putting the needs of our learners and communities first. Every effort we make is aimed at empowering students and ensuring their success.

Sustainability

Our programs are designed to create long-lasting impacts. By fostering a culture of continuous learning and growth, we aim to build a future where education thrives in every rural community.

Empowerment

We empower learners to rise above challenges and achieve their potential through quality education, mentorship, and skill-building opportunities.

Integrity

We uphold the highest standards of honesty and ethical conduct in every aspect of our work, ensuring trust and accountability to our learners and supporters.

Accountability

We take responsibility for the outcomes of our initiatives, ensuring transparency and commitment to our mission of educational excellence.

Transparency

Open communication and clear processes are at the heart of everything we do. We believe in building trust through honesty with our learners, donors, and partners.

Join Us to Shape a Brighter Future

At Masia Maths and Science Academy, we’re committed to providing exceptional STEM education to learners in Masia Village and surrounding communities. If you’re passionate about unlocking your child’s potential in Mathematics, Science, and Technology, our application process is the first step toward their brighter future.

How to Apply

Who Can Apply?

Learners from Grades 8 to 11 who demonstrate a passion for learning and academic excellence.

Maximum Capacity?

We accept a maximum of 90 students per grade to ensure quality education and personalized support.

Required Information?

During the application, you’ll need to provide the learner’s personal details, grade level, and guardian contact information.
