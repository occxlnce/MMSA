Core Programs

At Masia Maths and Science Academy, our programs are designed to inspire, educate, and empower learners with the knowledge and skills they need to excel in STEM fields. Here’s an overview of our key initiatives:

ICT Academy

Empowering Learners in the Digital Age

The ICT Academy equips learners with essential computer literacy skills, preparing them for the demands of the 4th Industrial Revolution. By providing individual access to state-of-the-art computer labs, we ensure that every learner is empowered to succeed in an increasingly digital world.

Key Highlights:
  • 780 learners trained in 2024 from 14 primary schools.
  • Training also extended to unemployed youth for broader community impact.
  • Focus on practical, hands-on sessions to build confidence in using technology.

MMSA Intervention Programme (MIP)

Bridging the Learning Gap

This program focuses on providing intensive academic support to learners in grades 8–12. It addresses critical learning gaps in Mathematics, Physical Sciences, Life Sciences, and English, ensuring that students receive the education they deserve.

Key Features:
  • Dedicated tutors for personalized guidance.
  • High demand: MMSA supported 365 learners in 2024, demonstrating the program’s value to the community.
  • Proven results: Increased pass rates and improved academic confidence among learners.

Career Programme

Building Futures Beyond the Classroom

This initiative empowers students with the knowledge and tools they need to plan for their futures. Through career guidance workshops, presentations, and excursions, MMSA helps learners explore opportunities in STEM fields and beyond.

Key Features:
  • Workshops on tertiary education, career exploration, and personal development.
  • Special support for out-of-school youth to find employment or re-enter education.
  • Guidance from experienced mentors and professionals in various fields.

STEM Girl Child Programme

Empowering Future Female Leaders in STEM

This program focuses on inspiring and mentoring young girls to pursue careers in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics. By providing exposure to female role models and facilitating hands-on workshops, MMSA is paving the way for a more inclusive STEM community.

Key Features:
  • Workshops and mentorship opportunities, especially during Women’s Month.
  • Focus on breaking gender stereotypes in STEM fields.
  • Inspires girls to take up leadership roles in technology and science.

External Special Programs

Encouraging Excellence Beyond the Classroom

MMSA provides learners with opportunities to participate in national and international STEM competitions, expos, and Olympiads. These activities broaden their horizons and inspire them to aim higher.

Key Features:
  • Competitions: Maths Olympiad, Eskom Expo for Young Scientists, MINTEK Quiz.
  • Expos: South African Water Prize, STEM expos, and more.
  • Opportunities: Platforms for learners to showcase their talents and gain recognition.

Maths Pioneers Programme (MPP)

Fostering a Love for Mathematics Early

MPP is designed for learners in grades 4–9, focusing on building a strong foundation in Mathematics. The program aims to challenge the perception that Math is difficult by making learning engaging and accessible.

Key Features:
  • Early intervention to build confidence in Mathematics.
  • Engaged 150 learners in 2024, ensuring they develop a passion for the subject.
  • Reinforces foundational skills to prepare students for advanced topics.

Making an Impact, One Program at a Time

Each program is tailored to meet the unique needs of rural learners, providing them with resources, mentorship, and opportunities to excel. Together, these initiatives form the foundation of MMSA’s mission to empower learners through STEM education.

About Us

We are a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering rural learners through innovative teaching and quality STEM education. Located in Masia Village, Limpopo, MMSA provides a beacon of hope for underprivileged learners who aspire to excel in Mathematics, Physical Sciences, and ICT.
